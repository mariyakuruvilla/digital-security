Recovery Principles

Dedicated Password Vault

Store encrypted passwords in Bitwarden or 1Password — never in browser autofill alone.

Hardware or TOTP 2FA

Prefer authenticator apps or FIDO2 keys over SMS to resist SIM-swapping attacks.

The 3-2-1 Backup Rule

Keep 3 copies of vital data on 2 different media formats, with 1 copy stored off-site.

Quarterly Access Audit

Review recovery emails, phone numbers, and trusted devices every 90 days.

About Security Assessments

The Risk Assessment section produces simulated, rule-based findings derived solely from the data you have entered in this application. It does not:

  • Connect to any external API, breach database, or threat intelligence feed
  • Scan your actual online accounts or network traffic
  • Perform real vulnerability detection or intrusion analysis
  • Store or transmit your data outside this browser window

All sample data included in this application is entirely fictional and is intended for educational demonstration purposes only.
